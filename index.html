Privacy Policy for Zyvo

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zyvo ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Zyvo food-ordering app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Google or email, Firebase Authentication is used to authenticate you. This may include your email address, name, and account identifier, depending on the sign-in method and information you provide.
  • Order and contact information: Information you provide to place an order, such as your name, phone number, delivery address, selected items, and order details.
  • Location information: If you grant location permission, the app may access your device's location to obtain GPS coordinates for delivery. You can control location permission through your device settings; some delivery features may not work without it.
  • Information stored in Firebase: Information submitted through the app may be stored in Google Firebase Firestore to support app functionality and order handling.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to create and manage accounts, show relevant delivery locations, process and coordinate orders, contact customers about orders, and operate and improve the app.

3. WhatsApp and Order Sharing

When you choose to continue an order through WhatsApp, information included in the order message—such as your contact details, delivery address or coordinates, and selected items—may be shared with the WhatsApp account receiving the order. Please review the message before sending it. WhatsApp is operated by a third party and its handling of information is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Payments

Zyvo does not currently process payments within the app. Any payment arrangement made through WhatsApp or another external channel takes place outside the app. Do not send payment information unless you are comfortable with the recipient and the payment method.

5. Service Providers and Sharing

We use Google Firebase Authentication for sign-in and Firebase Firestore to store and support app data. Information is shared with these services as needed to operate Zyvo. Information is also shared with the WhatsApp recipient when you choose to send an order through WhatsApp. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, manage orders, resolve issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices

You can manage location permission in your device settings and choose whether to send an order through WhatsApp. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Zyvo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children who are not permitted to use the service under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us
