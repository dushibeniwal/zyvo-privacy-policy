Privacy & account control

Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your Zyvo account and the personal data associated with it using the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Tap the button below to email the Zyvo support team.
  2. If possible, send the request from the email address associated with your Zyvo account.
  3. Include your account email and phone number, if one is linked to your account. Do not send your password or verification codes.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.
Email a deletion request

Or email us directly at zyvo666@gmail.com.

What happens next?

Our team will review the request, verify account ownership where necessary, and process eligible deletion requests. We may contact you if we need more information. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, or payment credentials in your email.

What data is deleted?

When a valid request is approved, we will delete the Zyvo account and associated personal data that we can delete, including applicable account information and customer data stored for the account. Some records may need to be retained for a limited period where required by law, to resolve disputes, or for security and fraud-prevention purposes. Any data retained for these reasons will be limited to what is necessary.

Need help?

For account deletion questions, contact zyvo666@gmail.com.

Important: Sending an email submits a request; it does not instantly delete your account. Zyvo support must verify and complete the request.
